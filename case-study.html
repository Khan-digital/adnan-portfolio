HatDu — Digital Request & Offer Platform

Designing a trusted digital platform where verified requesters and providers can connect, manage requests and offers, and collaborate through flexible account roles and permissions.

Product Designer · UX/UI · Digital Experience
Timeline
2022–2026
Product
Digital Request & Offer Platform
01 / Context

Designing for a product with real constraints.

HatDu connects people who need something with providers who can offer it. The product combines requests, offers, identity verification, account types and permission management into one digital experience.

My role has evolved across product design, UX/UI, visual design, interaction, motion and product communication. The focus has always been on making the platform clearer and easier to use while working within real business and technical constraints.

02 / The challenge

How do you make a complex system feel simple?

HatDu has multiple user types, workflows and levels of access. Users need to understand what they can do, what information they need to provide and what happens next.

Trust

Identity verification

Users need to be verified before they can participate in the request and offer system.

Accounts

Private & company users

The product supports different account contexts without making the experience unnecessarily complicated.

Permissions

Flexible access

Accounts can give other people access while maintaining control over roles and permissions.

Workflows

Requests & offers

Users need clear paths through creating requests, receiving offers and managing the resulting information.

03 / Product thinking

Small UX decisions can change the whole product.

Good product design doesn't make complex systems look complicated.

One example was the account experience. I proposed allowing users to work with requester and provider roles through one login rather than requiring separate email identities.

I also designed a “Copy & Publish Request” flow so users could reuse existing request information instead of rebuilding similar requests from scratch.

04 / Interface design

From structure to interface.

My work spans information hierarchy, responsive layouts, UI components, interaction patterns, typography and visual consistency.

05 / Beyond the interface

Product design doesn't stop at the screen.

Alongside product and UI work, I have created motion pieces, product demonstrations, onboarding content, social communication and educational material to help people understand how HatDu works.

These activities strengthened the connection between product experience, communication and visual design.

06 / Reflection

Designing inside a real product changes how you think.

Working on HatDu over several years has taught me to think beyond individual screens — considering systems, users, business requirements, iteration and the realities of shipping digital products.
