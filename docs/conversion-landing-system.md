# Conversion Landing System — Product 01

## Purpose
A focused Figma resource for building responsive, conversion-oriented landing pages without rebuilding common web patterns from scratch.

## Audience
- Freelance designers
- Small agencies
- Startup/product designers
- Small-business owners working with Figma

## Positioning
Quality and usability over block-count marketing. The system should feel like a small, coherent design system rather than a pile of unrelated sections.

## Component rules
- Use Auto Layout wherever content can change.
- Prefer Fill container for fluid children.
- Use Hug contents for buttons and content-driven elements.
- Use min/max widths where responsive behaviour needs guardrails.
- Components must have clear names and variants.
- Avoid fixed text boxes where content should reflow.
- Keep desktop and mobile behaviour intentional, not simply scaled.

## Quality gate before launch
- Every major section works with longer and shorter copy.
- Mobile layouts are tested.
- Components are reusable rather than duplicated.
- Layer names are understandable.
- Example pages can be assembled from the system.
- Documentation explains the system in minutes.
- Product preview images show real editable components, not fake marketing claims.
- No sales/purchase activation until legal and tax requirements are cleared.
