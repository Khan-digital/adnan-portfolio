# Free Sample — AI Design Workflow Pack

## Evidence-first synthesis

Use this prompt after pasting real research notes:

> Group the evidence into themes. For each theme, separate direct evidence, repeated patterns, contradictions and open questions. Do not create facts that are not present in the source material.

## UI review

> Review this interface for information hierarchy. Identify the first thing a user is likely to notice, the intended primary action, competing elements and anything that creates unnecessary cognitive load.

## Important
AI can accelerate exploration, but it should not replace evidence, design judgment or validation.
