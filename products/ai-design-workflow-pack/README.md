# AI Design Workflow Pack

A practical workflow system for designers using AI without giving up design judgment.

### What the full pack contains
- Project kickoff brief
- UX research & synthesis prompts
- User-flow and edge-case checklist
- UI review prompts
- Content & microcopy prompts
- Design critique framework
- Handoff & QA checklist
- Portfolio case-study story framework

### Positioning
Use AI for exploration, synthesis and acceleration — while keeping research evidence, design judgment and final decisions with the designer.
